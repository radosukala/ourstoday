## 0. Decision language

Every public document, interface, agent prompt and build receipt must use these
states consistently.

- **Adopted** — part of the confirmed direction until changed through the
  appropriate process.
- **Constitutional commitment** — a binding project constraint now and an
  intended legal/member right once ratified into an appropriate entity.
- **Hypothesis** — a mechanism that requires observed evidence.
- **Future decision** — intentionally unresolved because guessing would create
  false authority or premature infrastructure.
- **Concept data** — fictional information used to inspect a design. It must
  never appear as live evidence.
- **Observed evidence** — a sourced result from real use, operation or an
  auditable event.

Agents may not silently convert a hypothesis into an adopted decision.

### Primary message

### Thesis

> **OURS is a member-owned network that builds its own software in public.**

This sentence states the constitutional destination. Until a legal membership

> **OWNERSHIP: COMMITTED · LEGAL MEMBERSHIP: NOT YET ISSUED**

The status line protects the thesis from becoming a false present-tense legal

The first product is not a directory of ideas, an AI code editor, a generic

> **The first product is the network forming itself in public.**

Its first executable loop is:

> **enter → receive a permanent place → carry a relay → create a verified
> connection → participate → become eligible for legal membership**

The software exists to make that human sequence honest, legible, safe and
repeatable.

## 2. Why OURS exists

Software construction is becoming abundant. Coordination is not.

Incumbents retain power because people, relationships, histories, creators,
buyers, sellers and communities cannot move together merely because alternative
code exists. Attention can expose an alternative; attention cannot by itself
make the alternative useful.

OURS exists to own the scarce layer collectively:

- a network of people willing to act together;
- a record of how that network formed;
- conditional commitments to test, use and move;
- institutional memory of proposals, evidence and decisions;
- trusted stewardship and operating capability;
- the power to build, replace or retire software without losing the network.

The network is the durable institution. Products are replaceable vehicles.

## 4. The Founding Ledger

The ledger recognizes chronology and courage. It does not allocate capital,
status classes or extra sovereignty.

### Adopted rules

1. A verified entrant receives the next public number when their entry is
   durably sealed.
2. There is no number reservation and no countdown.
3. A number cannot be bought, sold, transferred or reassigned.
4. A person does not need an audience or successor to keep their place.
5. A click, impression, reaction or unverified signup never changes ledger
   state.
6. A successor activates a connection, not the predecessor's right to belong.
7. Referral count never creates additional votes.
8. The public Founding Ledger and the legal Member Register are separate
   records.
9. A public identity can be withdrawn or pseudonymized. “Permanent” means the
   place is not reassigned; it does not cancel privacy or erasure rights.
10. Corrections and withdrawals are new events. History is not silently edited.
11. The Founding Era contains exactly 1,000,000 places; the allocator rejects
    every later entry.
12. Each active place carries the equal, non-transferable project rights in
    [Founding Right 0.1](./FOUNDING-RIGHT-0.1.md). It is not legal membership or
    a share.

The complete lifecycle and race behavior are defined in the
[Founding Relay Protocol](./FOUNDING-RELAY-PROTOCOL.md).

## 5. The Founding Relay

Every sealed entrant receives a signed relay URL. When another verified person
enters through it, the connection becomes part of the formation record.

The first verified successor is the entrant's **First Continuation**. Further
people may still arrive through the same relay, but OURS does not publish a
referral leaderboard.

The relay creates two legitimate public moments:

1. “I entered the Founding Ledger as #000002.”
2. “The line continued. #000019 entered through #000002.”

The second event recognizes causality without creating a multilevel incentive.

### Hypotheses to test

- Whether First Continuation materially increases verified propagation.
- Whether passkey-first or email-first verification produces the better balance
  of access and integrity.
- Which share language creates conviction rather than spam.
- How to make the path from place #1,000,000 to Constitution 1.0 ratification
  short, understandable and independently verifiable.

No economic reward may be attached to these hypotheses before legal and
behavioral review.

## 6. Participation is a ladder, not a badge

| State | Meaning | What it does not mean |
|---|---|---|
| **Watcher** | Reads public formation, builds and decisions | No governance right |
| **Entrant** | Has a sealed Founding Million place and Founding Right 0.1 | Not a legal member or shareholder |
| **Continued** | Has a verified First Continuation connection | No extra vote or ownership |
| **Tester** | Completes a real product task and supplies evidence | A reaction is not a test |
| **Committer** | Makes an explicit conditional use or switch commitment | Not an unconditional promise |
| **Contributor** | Supplies verified work, expertise, adoption or responsibility | Credit is not a token |
| **Member-eligible** | Meets published constitutional and legal criteria | Eligibility is not issuance |
| **Member** | Holds an issued legal membership interest and accepts duties | Membership is not transferable influence |
| **Steward** | Holds a named, reviewable operational mandate | Stewardship is not unlimited sovereignty |

Every surface must show the actual state. “Founder,” “member,” “owner” and
“steward” cannot be cosmetic labels.

## 7. Proposals: outsource reach, never memory or authority

X, LinkedIn, blogs and other networks are distribution surfaces. They are not
the canonical memory or voting system of OURS.

Every proposal has one canonical case file on OURS containing:

- the problem and affected people;
- current state and proposed diff;
- evidence for and against;
- risks, costs and reversibility;
- structured commitments;
- linked external discussions;
- unresolved dissent;
- the responsible steward;
- the authority required for a decision;
- the result and subsequent evidence.

Native responses are structured actions:

- **I experience this problem**
- **I will test this**
- **I will switch if…**
- **I bring evidence**
- **I object because…**
- **I can steward this**

Likes, reposts and impressions are attention signals only. They cannot satisfy a
gate, select a cell, change the constitution or create governance power.

The full lifecycle is defined in the
[Proposal and Deliberation Protocol](./PROPOSAL-AND-DELIBERATION-PROTOCOL.md).

## 8. Daily public building

OURS TODAY is a finite daily instrument, not an infinite feed.

Every edition should expose four causal tapes:

1. **Formation Tape** — sealed entries, relay connections, withdrawals and
   membership events.
2. **Build Tape** — input, agent proposal, steward ruling, code diff, test and
   observed result.
3. **Constitution Diff** — current text, proposed text, authority and status.
4. **Next Decision** — the one consequential open case most ready for human
   participation.

A raw terminal replay is not sufficient. The public needs to see why a change
was attempted, what authority allowed it, what changed and what happened.

Every public build record must distinguish:

- shipped locally;
- deployed publicly;
- test passed;
- observed by real users;
- still a hypothesis;
- blocked on human or legal authority.

## 10. Governance

### Member-reserved authority

Once legal membership exists, members reserve authority over:

- mission and constitution;
- membership classes and rights;
- data, privacy and advertising rules;
- material surplus allocation;
- major capital, merger, sale or shutdown;
- election or ratification of stewards where defined;
- disposition of shared assets;
- cell secession and federation rules.

The adopted sovereignty principle is one member, one vote. Founding number,
wealth, contribution score, audience and referral count do not create extra
votes.

### Steward authority

Named stewards decide reversible daily product and operating questions inside
their published mandate and budget. Steward decisions require reasons and are
reviewable.

### Transitional authority

Before legal membership exists, the founder-steward may operate the project and
adopt reversible implementation decisions. Such decisions must be labeled
**FOUNDER-STEWARD DECISION — NOT MEMBER RATIFICATION**.

The founder cannot use transitional authority to claim that legal membership or
member ratification already exists.

The [Founding Constitution 0.1](./CONSTITUTION-0.1.md) defines the current
project charter and intended constitutional floor.

## 11. Ownership and economics

Member ownership must become enforceable rather than decorative.

The intended membership interest includes:

- one constitutional vote;
- access to governance and operating records;
- data portability and a right to leave;
- rights over major asset, data, advertising, merger and shutdown decisions;
- participation in surplus under transparent rules;
- defined duties, conflicts and exit conditions.

Until the legal entity and membership instrument exist, participation creates
history and the operative project rights in Founding Right 0.1—not ownership,
a share or a promise of profit.

### Adopted economic constraints

- No behavioral advertising.
- No sale of member or behavioral data.
- No token or tradable contribution credit.
- No payment for impressions, raw clicks or unretained signups.
- No multilevel referral incentives.
- Costs, reserves, stewardship compensation and surplus must be visible.
- Durable use must eventually cover durable operation.

Jurisdiction, entity form, membership instrument, capital rules, taxation and
surplus formula remain future legal decisions.

## 12. Privacy, identity and integrity

OURS separates:

- a public ledger identity or approved pseudonym;
- private verification and recovery data;
- later legal member identity;
- cell-specific permissions and roles.

Public formation must not require public legal identity. Formal governance may
require stronger one-person assurance than initial entry.

Personal data must be purpose-bound, exportable, correctable and erasable where
law requires. Public chronology must be designed so that an identity can be
withdrawn without reassigning its number or exposing private verification data.

The audit pattern may later use signed daily snapshots and append-only
consistency proofs. This is an integrity mechanism, not a currency or
blockchain.

## 13. Cells and the Mission Market

The cell architecture remains adopted. Its timing changes.

A cell is:

> **Mission × Thesis × Constituency × Institution**

The unit is a mission, not an idea. Ideas confer no status or right to consume
network resources.

The Mission Market does not lead Day 1. It returns only when at least two real
candidate cells have:

- named affected people;
- evidence beyond social reactions;
- a falsifiable thesis;
- verified testers or conditional committers;
- named stewardship;
- an honest operating and safety boundary.

Until then, a heatmap would be fictional supply. The only honest first
institutional build is OURS itself.

When cells exist, the market displays verified commitment, readiness, cost and
next unlock—not a universal speculative score.

## 14. Product topology

OURS is not committed to rebuilding every incumbent as a separate application,
nor to one super-app.

The adopted rule is architectural reversibility:

- the member network and constitutional memory sit above products;
- cells may share identity, ledger, proposal, payment and operating rails;
- one client may host multiple cells if evidence supports it;
- a cell may also ship an independent product;
- products and cells must remain exportable;
- no interface shell may become the new landlord of the network.

The network is the durable layer. Product topology is an evidence-led future
decision.

## 15. Roadmap and proof gates

### Phase A — Form the network

Build:

- Day 1 public instrument;
- canonical Founding Ledger;
- verification and withdrawal;
- signed relays and First Continuation;
- Formation Tape;
- transparent legal status;
- complete personal export.

Evidence required:

- entrants understand what they did and did not receive;
- the relay produces verified humans rather than click volume;
- abuse can be reviewed without centralizing unnecessary identity data;
- withdrawals and corrections work;
- at least some entrants return to perform a real act.

### Phase B — Let the network act

Build:

- canonical proposals;
- structured commitments and objections;
- external-source linking;
- agent Deliberation Digests;
- steward decisions and Change Packs;
- Build Tape and evidence receipts.

Evidence required:

- proposals become better specified;
- dissent survives summarization;
- stewards can decide without an unstructured engagement feed;
- real tests resolve questions that social popularity cannot.

### Phase C — Constitute ownership

Complete licensed legal work for:

- entity and jurisdiction;
- membership instrument;
- member register;
- voting and quorum;
- stewardship mandate;
- capital, surplus, exit and dissolution;
- privacy and data-controller obligations.

Only then issue legal membership and change the public status.

### Phase D — Form cells

Admit the first bounded mission only when it has a real constituency and
stewards. Run a concierge proof before generalizing cell infrastructure.
